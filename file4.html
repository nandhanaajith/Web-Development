ICET

Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET) is a private, self-financing engineering institution established in 2002 at Mulavoor, near Muvattupuzha in Ernakulam district, Kerala. Affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), the college offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in engineering, management, and computer applications.
